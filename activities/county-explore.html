Former Utah Territory Counties

  • Desert County — March 3, 1852–January 17, 1862; its territory became parts of Box Elder and Tooele counties and Nevada.
  • Green River County — March 3, 1852–February 16, 1872; its former territory is now divided among several Utah counties, Wyoming, and Colorado.
  • Carson County — January 17, 1854–March 2, 1861; became part of Nevada.
  • Cedar County — January 5, 1856–January 17, 1862; became part of Utah County.
  • Greasewood County — January 5, 1856–January 17, 1862; became part of Box Elder County.
  • Humboldt County — January 5, 1856–March 2, 1861; became part of Nevada.
  • Malad County — January 5, 1856–January 17, 1862; became part of Box Elder County.
  • St. Mary’s County — January 5, 1856–January 17, 1862; became part of Nevada.
  • Shambip County — January 12, 1856–January 17, 1862; became part of Tooele County.
  • Rio Virgen County — February 18, 1869–February 16, 1872; its territory became parts of Washington County, Nevada, and Arizona.
